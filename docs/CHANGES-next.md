# Unreleased (after 2.6.2)
- `--germline-pop FILE`: germline variation of a population. A VCF of sites with the allele frequency (`INFO/AF`, as in gnomAD or 1000 Genomes) gives the genotype of one individual at every biallelic SNV of the chromosome, drawn with Hardy-Weinberg proportions (heterozygous alleles on a random haplotype; a new individual in every replicate; sites independent, no linkage disequilibrium). Used with `--fasta`; not with `-c` or `--germline-vcf`. The sites are flagged `GERMLINE`. Without the option nothing changes.
- **Structured coalescent with colonizations (`--demes`)**: the cells are sampled from tumor regions (demes): the primary tumor (deme 1) and metastases that are founded once, at a given time and by a given number of cells of a source deme, and then grow apart (no exchange of cells between demes: a colonization model, not a migration one). `--deme-n 20:10:10` (cells per deme; `-s` is ignored), `--deme-time` (colonization times, in N generations), `--deme-source` (which deme founds which; metastasis-to-metastasis seeding is possible), `--deme-founders` (1 = monoclonal, more = polyclonal seeding) and `--deme-size` (relative size of each deme). `demes.tsv` lists the deme of every cell. The coalescence times agree with the theory (checked in `tests/run_tests.sh`). Cannot be combined with growth, demographic periods or a user tree. Without the options nothing changes.
- **Indels in the reads mode (`--indel-rate`)**: small insertions and deletions that happen on the branches of the genealogy and are inherited by the cells; the reads that overlap them carry them, with a CIGAR with `I`/`D` (and soft clips). `--indel-rate R` (relative to the substitution rate `-u`), `--indel-ins` (fraction of insertions), `--indel-len` (mean length) and `--indel-max` (maximum length, up to 32). The indels are left-aligned and their truth is in `reads_<n>/indels_truth.vcf` (VCF with the genotype of every cell). Works with `--blocks`/`--fasta`, doublets and any tile size or number of threads; it cannot be combined with segment losses/gains yet. Without the option nothing changes.
