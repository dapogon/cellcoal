# Unreleased (after 2.6.1)
- `--damage SPEC` (reads mode): damage of the library before the PCR, as `FROM>TO:RATE` items (`G>T:0.002,C>T:0.001`). The change is in one strand of the molecule, so it is seen in only one orientation of the reads (orientation bias of oxidation and deamination) and the PCR duplicates inherit it; it also affects the healthy bulk. Without the option nothing changes.
- **Reads mode with a memory that does not depend on the genome.** The genome of every cell is simulated in tiles of 262,144 bases and the BAM records that are complete after each tile are written at once (before, all the records of a cell were kept until the end); the per-site read-count structures, which the reads mode does not use, are not allocated any more. A genome of 4 Mb with 4 cells at 30X needs 0.96 GB instead of 2.4 GB, and a chromosome of 20 blocks of 200 kb with 8 cells, 87 MB. Every cell and haplotype has its own random stream that goes on from tile to tile, so the BAM files are the same for any size of tile (`CELLCOAL_READ_TILE`, for tests) and any number of threads. **The reads of a given seed are different from those of 2.6.1** (the order in which the molecules are generated changed; same statistics); the molecules are numbered (`MI`) by haplotype, and the order of the FASTQ records is by tile.
