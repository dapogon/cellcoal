# CellCoal (next version) — what changed

- Clones of different fitness: `--clones n=N,time=...,parent=...,fitness=...,founders=...,death=...` (options `--clone-n`, `--clone-time`, `--clone-parent`, `--clone-fitness`, `--clone-founders`, `--clone-death`). The tumor is one mixed population of clones that grow at different rates (the fitness is the growth rate relative to the clone 1, `-g`); the size of every clone today follows from its age, its growth and its founders, and the `-s` cells are sampled at random from the whole tumor, so the number of cells of every clone changes among replicates (`clones.tsv`). The genealogy is the structured coalescent of the demes with a size that grows in every clone, and a coalescence rate 2 b / N(t) (`death=0.5` gives the coalescent of Slatkin and Hudson). Checked against a forward simulation of the whole tumor (`tests/clones_check.py`); the sizes of the clones are fixed numbers (deterministic limit), see the manual (section 5.1.4).
- The output of the runs without `--clones` is identical to the previous version (golden outputs).
