# CellCoal (next version) — what changed

- Clones of different fitness: `--clones n=N,time=...,parent=...,fitness=...,founders=...` (options `--clone-n`, `--clone-time`, `--clone-parent`, `--clone-fitness`, `--clone-founders`). The tumor is one mixed population of clones that grow at different rates (the clone 1 is the neutral tumor, with the birth rate `-K` and the death rate `-L` of Ohtsuki and Innan, or `-g`; the fitness of the other clones is their growth rate relative to it); the size of every clone today follows from its age, its growth and its founders, and the `-s` cells are sampled at random from the whole tumor, so the number of cells of every clone changes among replicates (`clones.tsv`). The genealogy is the structured coalescent of the demes with a size that grows in every clone, and the coalescence rate of the global model chosen (2 b / N(t) with `-K -L`, 1 / N(t) with `-g`). Checked against a forward simulation of the whole tumor (`tests/clones_check.py`); the sizes of the clones are fixed numbers (deterministic limit), see the manual (section 5.1.4).
- The output of the runs without `--clones` is identical to the previous version (golden outputs).
